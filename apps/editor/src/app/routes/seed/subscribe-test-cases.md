# Subscribe-block test matrix

Written for: developers and reviewers checking subscribe-block and member-plan
behaviour on a review system.

Generated by the seed route at `/seed` (see `seed.tsx`). Every case below
becomes one published page whose slug is listed, containing exactly one
subscribe block. Open `/t-index` on the website for a linked list of every
generated page.

The matrix is built from four dimensions. Cases are the cross-product of them
plus curated multi-plan, block-feature and hostile-configuration groups —
**245 published pages** from **20 member plans** in total.

## Group A — the cube (144 pages)

`3 display modes × 8 layouts × 6 core archetypes`, one plan per block.

Slug: `t-cube-<display>-<layout>-<plan>`

What to look for:

- **`dropdown` + any layout**: every price must read as monthly. The card must
  print "Ab" only when a higher amount is actually selectable — so `none` shows
  a bare price, `picker-*` without a free input shows "Ab <lowest tile>", and
  `slider`/`*-input` show "Ab <min>".
- **`dropdown` + `yearly-only-offered`**: the plan must be dimmed, labelled
  "Für dieses Zahlungsintervall nicht verfügbar", and the submit button
  disabled (it is the only plan in the block).
- **`offercards`**: one card per plan × interval; the interval subtitle appears
  only when the plan offers more than one.
- **`toggle`**: the interval toggle sits above the cards and the cards reprice;
  the selected toggle button must match the selected card's blue.
- **`none-input`**: the spinner replaces the separate amount section, shows that
  plan's target-or-minimum until selected, and the price line follows it.
- **`picker-periods` + `three-intervals`**: tile values must differ per interval
  in `offercards`/`toggle`, and collapse to the monthly tab in `dropdown`.

## Group B — pricing archetypes (60 pages)

All 20 archetypes × 3 display modes, layout fixed to `picker-input`.

Slug: `t-plan-<plan>-<display>`

What to look for: currency symbol and position (`eur`), the `Gratis` branch
(`free`), absence of a period suffix (`non-extendable`), the preselected
interval (`default-yearly`), lifetime wording, and that `no-methods` does not
crash the picker.

## Known-good expectations recorded this session

These are the behaviours the matrix is meant to protect:

1. In **Auswahlfeld** every price is monthly and pinned — it does not follow the
   selected interval. The interval only fills the select, labels the submit
   button and goes into the payload.
2. **"Ab"** appears only when a higher amount can actually be selected, and the
   advertised figure is the lowest _selectable_ amount, not `amountMin`.
3. A plan that does not offer the displayed interval is **dimmed, disabled and
   unsellable**; if no plan qualifies, the form cannot be submitted.
4. `Fixer Betrag` charges `amountTarget ?? amountMin`.
5. A selected plan without a stored layout is served as `Fixer Betrag` by the
   **server**, not as "no layout" — see §6.6.3 of the team doc.

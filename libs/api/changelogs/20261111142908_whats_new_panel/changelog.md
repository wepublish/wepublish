---
title: "What's new" moves into its own panel
lead: The changelog no longer fills your dashboard. Open it with the sparkle icon at the bottom of the sidebar; a dot tells you when something new arrived.
severity: info
---

- News show up as a short list of headings grouped by month. Click one to read the details right there.
- Changelog tasks now come in two levels: **Action recommended** (nothing breaks while it waits) and **Action required** (please do it soon). Only people who can complete them see them: as a "To do" section in the panel and as one line on the dashboard.
- An open required task also shows a slim red banner at the top of the page, which you can snooze until tomorrow. It never appears in the article and page editors.
- The full changelog is still on the notifications page.

---
title: Reactivate deactivated subscriptions
lead: A deactivated subscription is no longer a dead end: you can switch it back on directly in the editor. You can also undo a plan upgrade as long as it has not been paid.
actionRequired: false
---

**Reactivating a subscription**

- In the subscription list, deactivated subscriptions are now marked with an icon next to the membership plan and have a new "Reactivate" button.
- The same button is available on the subscription detail page.
- Before anything happens, a confirmation window shows the membership plan, the amount, the paid-until date and the reason for the deactivation.
- Reactivating removes the deactivation and the subscription simply continues. If the paid period has already run out, a new invoice is created automatically.

**Undoing an upgrade**

If a subscriber moved to a different plan and the new subscription has not been paid yet, you will see an "Undo upgrade" button. It deletes the new subscription together with its invoices and lets the previous subscription continue as before. Once the new subscription has been paid, the option disappears.

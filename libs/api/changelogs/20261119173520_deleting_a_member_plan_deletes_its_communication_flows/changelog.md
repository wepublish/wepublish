---
title: Deleting a member plan deletes its communication flows
lead: When you delete a member plan, the communication flows of that plan are now deleted with it. Before, they stayed behind without a plan, and from then on no automatic message went out at all.
actionRequired: false
---

If a member plan was deleted earlier and automatic messages have not been sent since, this update removes the flows it left behind, and sending works again. The default flow and the flows of other member plans stay as they are.

---
title: A video library
lead: You can now upload video files to We.Publish and manage them under Files, the same way you already manage images and documents.
---

Until now the only way to put a video on your site was to embed one hosted somewhere else — YouTube, Vimeo and the rest. Those still work exactly as before. This adds a home for video you host yourself.

**Files › Videos**

- Upload MP4, WebM, OGG, MOV, M4V and MPEG files, up to 500 MB each.
- Give a video a title and a description, search the list by title, and copy its public link.
- Open a video to play it straight away, so you can check you uploaded the right file.
- The library shows how much storage you have used, as the document library does.

Embedded videos are unchanged: keep using the YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook and TikTok blocks for anything hosted elsewhere.

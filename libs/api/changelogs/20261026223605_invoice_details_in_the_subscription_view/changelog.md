---
title: More invoice details in the subscription view
lead: The invoice table of a subscription now shows when each invoice was paid. More details, such as the payment method and payment status, can be switched on in the column menu.
actionRequired: false
---

- **Paid on:** The invoice table in a subscription shows the date each invoice was paid. Unpaid invoices show a dash.
- **More details on request:** Open the **Columns** menu at the top left of the table to switch on further columns: paid via (payment method), payment status, marked as paid by, canceled on, deactivation if unpaid, created on, last modified, invoice email and items. They are hidden at first so the table stays compact; your choice is remembered in your browser.
- **Imported invoices** were paid in the previous system, so they show no payment method or payment status.
- **Columns hidden by default in other tables too:** Tables can now offer extra columns that start hidden. Switch them on in the **Columns** menu; **Reset columns** hides them again.

---
title: Download lists as CSV or Excel
lead: Most lists in the editor now have a Download button that saves all matching entries as a CSV or Excel file.
actionRequired: false
---

The download respects your current setup:

- **All entries, not just the current page.** Every row that matches your filters and search is included, in the current sort order.
- **Only the columns you see.** Columns you have hidden are left out of the file.
- **Dates stay dates.** In Excel files, dates are real date cells, so you can sort and filter them.

The button is available on articles, pages, comments, users, events, polls (votes), discount codes and their usage, goodies, user consents, invoices, API tokens and the audit log.

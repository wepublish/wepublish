---
title: Mailchimp reports cleaned and unsubscribed contacts right away
lead: One click in the Mailchimp sync settings registers a webhook at Mailchimp, so a contact that bounced or unsubscribed changes its email status in We.Publish within seconds instead of with the next nightly sync.
actionRequired: true
---

- In Settings, Integrations, Mailchimp sync, a new section "Reports from the audience" shows whether the webhook is registered and when Mailchimp last reported. Press "Register webhook at Mailchimp" once. We.Publish registers it with your API key; nothing has to be pasted into Mailchimp.
- The nightly sync keeps reading the audience as before, so nothing is lost if a report does not arrive.

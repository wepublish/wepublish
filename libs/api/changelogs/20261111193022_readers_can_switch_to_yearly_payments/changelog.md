---
title: Readers can switch to yearly payments
lead: Readers who pay their subscription monthly (or quarterly, half-yearly) can now switch to paying once a year in their profile. The switch applies from the next renewal; the current period stays as it is.
actionRequired: false
---

On the subscription card in the profile and on the subscription's own page, readers see **Switch to yearly payments** when their member plan offers yearly payment with their current payment method. A confirmation shows from which date they pay how much once a year instead of their current amount.

- Nothing changes until the paid period ends. An invoice already created for the next period is replaced by a yearly one.
- The reader keeps their amount. Only if the plan has a higher minimum for yearly payment is the amount raised to it, and the dialog says so.
- The switch is not offered for cancelled or unpaid subscriptions, during a payment method change, or for subscriptions billed by Payrexx Subscriptions.
- Every switch is recorded with the previous and the new periodicity and amount.

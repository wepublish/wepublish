---
title: Renewal confirmations now go out for every paid renewal
lead: Members receive the "renewal worked" mail as soon as a renewal invoice is paid — no matter how it was paid. When you mark an invoice as paid yourself, you can now decide whether the confirmation should be sent.
actionRequired: false
---

Until now the confirmation was only sent when the nightly job collected the amount automatically. If a member paid through a payment link or you marked the invoice as paid in the editor, no mail went out.

What changes:

- The confirmation is sent whenever a renewal invoice is paid: collected automatically, paid by the member themselves, or marked as paid by the editorial team.
- In the invoice list of a subscription, the "mark as paid" dialog now has the option **"Do not send a payment confirmation to the member"** — useful for corrections or bookings you don't want to announce.
- For the very first period of a new subscription no confirmation is sent; new members keep getting the welcome mail instead. The dialog tells you when this is the case.
- Each invoice triggers the confirmation at most once, so members never receive it twice for the same renewal.

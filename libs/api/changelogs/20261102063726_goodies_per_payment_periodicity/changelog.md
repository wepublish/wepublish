---
title: Goodies per payment periodicity
lead: A goodie can now be limited to certain payment periodicities, for example a booklet that only comes with a 2-year subscription.
actionRequired: false
---

In the goodie form you can pick the payment periodicities the goodie is offered with. Leave the field empty to keep offering the goodie with every periodicity of its subscription plans.

On the website the goodie only appears once the reader has chosen one of those periodicities, and the API refuses a goodie that does not match the chosen periodicity.

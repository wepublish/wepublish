---
title: Faster websites, changes live within seconds
lead: Your website now shares its pages and data across all of its servers. Pages load faster, and what you publish or change shows up on the website within seconds.
---

**What you will notice**

- **Publishing is visible almost immediately.** An article you publish, edit or unpublish is updated on its own page within a few seconds, and on the front page, lists and other pages shortly after. Scheduled articles and pages go live at their time.
- **Navigation, banners and settings** take effect on the whole website within seconds of saving.
- **Faster pages for your readers**, because a page is rendered once and then served by every server instead of each one rendering it again.

**Unpublished content**

A page or article that is not published (or no longer is) now shows readers a note that the content is not available and tells search engines not to list it. The preview for you and your team works as before, including the preview for external readers if you have switched that on.

**More secure logins**

- Changing your password logs you out on all your other devices. When an editor resets someone's password, all of that person's sessions end.
- Deactivating a user or changing their roles takes effect within seconds.
- A two-factor code can only be used once. After five wrong codes, two-factor login is locked for 15 minutes.

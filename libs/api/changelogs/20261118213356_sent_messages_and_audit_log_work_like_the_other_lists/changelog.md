---
title: Sent messages and audit log work like the other lists
lead: The lists under Sent messages and Audit log now page through reliably, keep their filters behind the Filters button and remember your settings, like every other list in the editor.
actionRequired: false
---

**Sent messages** (tab **Mails**):

- **Paging fixed.** Moving between pages no longer shows some mails twice and skips others, and the page size (10, 20, 50 or 100 per page) now takes effect.
- **Filters folded away.** Send job, template, state, delivery and trigger are behind the **Filters** button, which shows how many are active; the ✕ next to it resets them.
- **Remembered.** Filters, page size and the columns you show stay as you left them the next time you open the list in this browser.
- **New:** choose and reorder columns, and export the list as a spreadsheet.
- Opening the mails of a send from **Send jobs** or after a send still shows exactly that send. Changing a filter takes over from there.

**Audit log**: the filters are behind the **Filters** button too, and filters and page size are remembered the same way.

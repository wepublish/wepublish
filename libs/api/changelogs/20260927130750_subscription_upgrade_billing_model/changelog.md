---
title: Choose how subscription upgrades are billed
lead: A new "Subscription Plans" card in the settings lets you decide whether an upgrade charges the full price difference between the old and the new plan, or only credits the unused part of the current period.
actionRequired: false
---

You can find the new option under **Settings → Subscription Plans**:

- **Off (default):** nothing changes. When someone upgrades, the unused remainder of their current period is credited, so the amount depends on how much time is left.
- **On:** the upgrade always costs the difference between the new plan price and the current plan price, no matter when in the period the upgrade happens.

On the website, the upgrade form now also takes over the amount from the plan your readers pick, so the displayed price matches the selected plan right away.

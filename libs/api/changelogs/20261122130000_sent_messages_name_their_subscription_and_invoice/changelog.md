---
title: Sent messages name the subscription and invoice they are about
lead: The list of sent messages now shows which subscription and which invoice a message was about, and the recipient filter can use this, for example to find everyone who was reminded about an invoice that is still open.
actionRequired: false
---

**In the list of sent messages.** A new column **About** links to the subscription a message was sent for and says when it concerned an invoice. The same column appears in the message history of a user. Messages without such a reason, a login link for example, show a dash.

**In the recipient filter.** Each condition under **Messages received** has two new options:

- **Only messages about this very subscription**: for audiences of subscriptions. A person with two subscriptions is then only selected with the subscription the message was actually about.
- **Only messages about an invoice that is still open**: keeps people whose reminded invoice is neither paid nor cancelled. Once the invoice is paid, the person drops out of the audience.

Both options are available in **Manual messages** and in the manual assignment of the delivery channel.

**Good to know.** The subscription and invoice are recorded from this update on. Messages sent earlier keep no such reference and never meet the two new conditions; only earlier manual sends to subscribers are linked to their subscription afterwards.

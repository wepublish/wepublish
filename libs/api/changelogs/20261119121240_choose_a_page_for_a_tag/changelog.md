---
title: Show a page instead of a tag's article list
lead: Pick a page for an article tag, and your website shows that page at the tag's address instead of the list of articles with that tag.
actionRequired: false
---

When you edit an article tag, you can now choose a **Tag page**. Visitors who
open the tag on your website (for example by clicking it on an article) then see
that page instead of the automatic list of articles with this tag. This lets you
build a proper landing page for a topic, with an introduction and hand-picked
content.

Leave the field empty to keep showing the article list. As long as the chosen
page is not published, the website keeps showing the article list too.

Every We.Publish update now comes with a short changelog entry that explains in plain language what changes for you — in German, English and French.

- The **Notifications** section on the dashboard shows what needs your attention right now, together with the latest news.
- The new **Notifications** page in the sidebar keeps the full history, organised in tabs: What's new, We.Publish team messages and job logs.
- Use **Mark as read** to tidy up your dashboard — this only affects your own account, not your colleagues.
- Some updates require a manual step. These stay highlighted on the dashboard until someone on your team marks them as **done**, which confirms it for the whole team.
- Technical job alerts now only appear when something actually went wrong.

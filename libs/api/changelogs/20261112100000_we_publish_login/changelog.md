**What you will notice**

- **Your own login stays as it is.** The new link sits below the usual login and changes nothing for you and your team.
- **Only We.Publish staff can use it**, and only with two-factor authentication and an explicit permission for your editor.
- **We.Publish staff accounts are managed by We.Publish.** They are administrators, their names come from We.Publish, they cannot be changed, deactivated or deleted in your editor, and they can only sign in with this link, not with a password or a login link.
- **Every sign-in is recorded** in your editor's audit log.
- **When you ask us for help, our support can see your editor or website as one of your users would**, for example to check a subscription. This is only possible for a few We.Publish staff, needs a reason, lasts at most an hour and is shown while it runs. Everything done in such a session is marked in your audit log; the filter "Impersonation only" shows it.

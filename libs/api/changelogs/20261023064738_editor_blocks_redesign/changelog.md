---
title: Redesigned content blocks, teaser editing and publish dialog
lead: Most content blocks in the article and page editor have a cleaner, more consistent look, and the teaser and publish panels have been reworked to be clearer and easier to use.
actionRequired: false
---

- **Blocks:** Poll, crowdfunding, comment, event, embed, HTML, quote, gallery, newsletter form and subscribe blocks have a tidier layout. Blocks such as poll, crowdfunding, comments, events, embeds and HTML now appear as their own card with a clear edit button.
- **Empty slots:** Empty blocks and teaser slots now show a labelled placeholder (for example "Choose teaser") that you can click anywhere on.
- **Teasers:** Teaser grids, flex grids, teaser lists and teaser slots have been redesigned. On small tiles, actions are collected in a "More actions" menu. When editing a teaser, you now see a live preview; leave a field empty to use the original text. The teaser selection drawer has a title search.
- **Crowdfunding block:** Shows the name and progress of the selected crowdfunding at a glance.
- **Block templates:** Work better on small screens and remind you that changes to the template appear automatically.
- **Publishing:** The publish dialog is clearer, with a checklist "Before you publish" and a separate options section. The version history groups entries by "Today" and "Yesterday".
- **Fixes:** The image block respects its disabled state, and the listicle no longer cuts off its image picker.

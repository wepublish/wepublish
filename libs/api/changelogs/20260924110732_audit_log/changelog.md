title: New audit log — who changed what, and when
lead: A new Audit log page under Settings records every editorial change made in the editor, together with the person who made it and the login session it happened in. It is available to administrators.
actionRequired: false
---

Whenever someone creates, changes or deletes something in the editor — an article, a page, a user, a member plan — the audit log keeps a record of it. This makes it possible to answer questions like "who published this yesterday evening?" long after the fact.

- Find it under **Settings → Audit log** in the sidebar. Only administrators see the page.
- Each entry names **who** acted, **what** they did, **which record** it concerned and **when** it happened.
- If someone acted on behalf of another account, the entry names **both** people, so a change is never attributed to the wrong person.
- **Search** across actions, records and email addresses, or narrow the list down by action, record type, time range, failed attempts or borrowed accounts.
- Every entry shows the **login session** it belongs to. One click on it shows everything else that happened in that same session.
- Attempts that were **rejected** are recorded as well — an unsuccessful deletion is often more interesting than a successful one.

The log records **only** who did what and when. It never stores the content that was written or changed, and never passwords or access tokens. Entries are kept for twelve months and then removed automatically.

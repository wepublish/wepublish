---
title: Video and audio libraries
lead: You can now upload video and audio files to We.Publish and manage them under Files, the same way you already manage images and documents.
---

Until now the only way to put a video on your site was to embed one hosted somewhere else — YouTube, Vimeo and the rest. Those still work exactly as before. This adds a home for media you host yourself.

**Files › Videos**

- Upload MP4, WebM, OGG, MOV, M4V and MPEG files, up to 500 MB each.
- Open a video to play it straight away, so you can check you uploaded the right file.
- The list shows a still from each video, its dimensions and its bitrate. The still is grabbed in your browser as you upload, so nothing is sent anywhere twice.

**Files › Audio**

- Upload MP3, OGG, WAV, WebM, AAC, M4A and FLAC files, up to 200 MB each.
- Open an audio file to listen to it in place.
- The list shows the bitrate of each file.

Both libraries work the same way: give a file a title and a description, note where it came from under **Attribution** (source, link, licence), search the list by title, copy its public link, and see how much storage you have used.

Embedded videos are unchanged: keep using the YouTube, Vimeo, Facebook and TikTok blocks for anything hosted elsewhere.

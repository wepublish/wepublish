---
title: Date picker in dialogs works again
lead: The calendar of a date field in a dialog, such as the publish date when publishing or scheduling an article or page, no longer stays hidden, and the time can be typed again.
actionRequired: false
---

- The calendar of a date field in a dialog sometimes opened invisibly behind the dialog, usually after another date field had been used before, so the date could not be picked until the editor was reloaded. It now always shows on top.
- Clicking into the time field at the bottom of the calendar moved the cursor onto the selected day, so nothing could be typed. The time field now keeps the cursor, in dialogs, side panels and on normal pages.

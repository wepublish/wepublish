---
title: Message templates now have a version per delivery channel
lead: A template can now say something different by email and by letter. "Mail templates" are called "Message templates" from now on, and the edit page has been tidied up.
actionRequired: false
---

**One template, a version per channel**

If printed letters are switched on for your medium, a message template now has a tab per delivery channel: **Email** and **Letter**. Each version has its own subject and its own text, so a letter no longer has to reuse the wording of the email.

- **Add letter version** starts the letter as a copy of the email, ready to adapt.
- Stationery and additional pages (PDF) belong to the letter version and show on its tab only.
- Each tab has its own preview. A test mail is sent from the email tab.
- **Remove this version** asks before it removes anything.
- Templates for account messages (login link, password reset, email change) stay email only.

Your existing templates keep working as before. A template that already went out as a letter has received a letter version with the same text as its email.

**What this means for sending**

- A person who receives letters gets the letter version. If a template has no letter version, that person gets the email instead.
- On **Manual messages**, the delivery channel follows the template you pick: a channel the template has no version for cannot be chosen.
- Automated messages and system mails need a template with an email version.

**Also new on the edit page**

- The purposes have shorter names; what each one is for is explained in the ⓘ next to it.
- The placeholders are grouped under headings such as "Person" and "User", and the search field filters them.
- The HTML view is readable in dark mode.

**Fixed:** switching "Also by letter" on or off for an automated message no longer removes its template.

---
title: Letters behind a feature toggle
lead: Everything about printed letters – the letter and PDF integrations, stationery, the letter preview and the letter channel of the communication tool – now only appears once the "paper-letters" feature is switched on for your medium.
actionRequired: true
---

**What changes**

- The integrations **Letter provider** and **PDF renderer**, the **Stationery** page, "Preview as letter" in the mail templates and the **Letter** channel in the communication tool are hidden while the feature is off.
- Existing settings, stationery and templates are kept; nothing is deleted.

**What to do**

If your medium already sends letters, ask We.Publish to switch on the **paper-letters** feature for your medium. Until then, no letters can be sent.

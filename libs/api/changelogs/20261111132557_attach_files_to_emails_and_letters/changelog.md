---
title: Attach files to emails and letters
lead: Attach a Word document to a mail template and it is sent with every email as a filled-in PDF, or printed after the pages of a letter.
actionRequired: false
---

When you edit a mail template, you can now attach one or more Word documents
(.docx). Each document is filled in with the same placeholders as the mail
itself, converted to a PDF and sent along with every email from that template.
For readers who receive letters, the PDF is printed after the letter pages.

A document that is still attached to a template can no longer be deleted, so a
mail never loses its attachment without anyone noticing.

A template can attach up to five documents, 15 MB together. Templates used as
system mails, such as the login link or the password reset, cannot carry
attachments, so those mails never wait for a document to be converted.

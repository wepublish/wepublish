---
title: Type the time in date fields
lead: The clock in every date and time field is now a single field you type into, instead of two dropdowns. Any minute can be set, not just every fifth one.
actionRequired: false
---

- The time reads `HH:MM` and corrects itself as you type: `930` becomes `09:30`, and an hour or minute that cannot exist is pulled back into range.
- Minutes are no longer limited to five-minute steps.
- The shortcut buttons at the bottom of the calendar (**Today**, **Tomorrow**, **Now**, **OK**) now work in fields that sit inside a side panel, where they previously did nothing.

---
title: Exact prices per payment interval
lead: Member plans now have their own minimum, target and maximum price for every payment interval, so yearly or quarterly prices no longer have to be derived from the monthly amount. In the subscribe block you also decide how the intervals are presented to your readers.
actionRequired: false
---

**In the member plan**

- Under “Prices per enabled payment interval” you set the minimum, target and optional maximum amount for the whole period of each interval you have enabled — for example an exact yearly price instead of twelve times the monthly one.
- Each interval can carry an optional marketing label such as “2 months for free”, shown on the subscribe page.
- The discount compared to the extrapolated monthly price is calculated and displayed automatically.
- A new “Default payment interval” setting defines which interval is preselected on the subscribe page.
- The payment settings panel is now called “Edit payment methods & periodicities”: intervals you enable there are the ones you can price.

**In the subscribe block**

- New setting “Payment interval display” with three options: a select field (all prices shown monthly, the interval only decides how often it is billed), one card per plan and interval with its exact price, or a toggle above the cards that switches all prices to the chosen interval.
- Preset tile amounts can now be defined per payment interval.
- New checkbox to decide whether the goodie minimum amount also applies to upgrades.

**In the subscription editing view**

- The amount is now entered per payment interval; for non-monthly subscriptions the monthly equivalent is shown below the field.

Existing plans keep their current prices: the previous monthly amounts are carried over automatically as the monthly interval price.

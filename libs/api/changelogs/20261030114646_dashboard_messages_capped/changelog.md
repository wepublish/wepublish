---
title: A tidier notifications card on the dashboard
lead: The dashboard now shows at most four notifications, with every kind of message still represented, and a link takes you to the rest.
actionRequired: false
---

- Each kind of notification (connector alerts, team messages, changelog news and tasks, job errors) keeps at least one spot, so a flood of failed jobs can no longer push a team message off the dashboard.
- Only the most important notification shows its full text; the others show just their title and actions.
- When there are more, a link such as "+3 more notifications" opens the notifications page with everything.

title: We.Publish Support Login
lead: Below the login form there is now a "We.Publish Support Login" button. It lets our support team sign in to your editor when you ask us for help, without you having to share a password.
---

**What you will notice**

- **Your own login stays as it is.** The new button sits below the usual login and changes nothing for you and your team.
- **Only We.Publish staff can use it.** Signing in requires a We.Publish staff account with two-factor authentication. The support login is used only for your medium and lasts at most one hour.
- **Every support login is recorded**, also in your editor's audit log: who signed in, when and as whom.

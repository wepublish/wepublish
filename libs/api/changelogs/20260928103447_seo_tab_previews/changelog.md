- **Previews** update live while you edit the SEO and social media texts or change the share image. The Google preview is in the SEO tab, the social media previews in the social media tab. For X, both the large image card used by the website and the smaller summary card are shown.
- **Checklist for this content**: shows which points of the SEO checklist are met, for example the length of the SEO title and description, subheadings in longer texts or the size of the share image.
- **SEO analysis**: shows key figures of the content, such as the number of words, subheadings, links and images without a description.

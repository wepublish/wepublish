---
title: Ask monthly subscribers to switch to yearly payments
lead: A new engagement preset asks readers who pay monthly to pay once a year instead. Its button takes them straight to their own subscription, where they confirm the switch.
actionRequired: false
---

Pick **Switch monthly to yearly** under Audience Engagement. It shows a banner to the new audience **Readers who pay monthly**: readers with a running subscription they pay monthly and haven't cancelled. Write your own title and text, for example why a year of support helps you plan.

The button **Switch to yearly payments** opens the reader's monthly subscription with the confirmation. The switch applies from the next renewal. A reader who closes the banner won't see it again for 30 days. You can use the audience and the button in any other rule too.

---
title: Latest activity is back, and you can arrange your dashboard
lead: The dashboard shows the latest activity again, including new comments. You can now choose which cards you see and in which order.
---

**Latest activity**

The «Latest Activity» card is back as a horizontal timeline: new articles, pages, comments, subscriptions, users, authors, polls and events, newest on the left. Each entry shows its details on one line and opens its edit page when clicked; if a text is cut off, hover over the entry to read it in full – comments show what was written. The arrows at the top right move one entry at a time; keep an arrow pressed to scroll continuously. With the filter button next to the arrows you choose which kinds of activity you want to see, for example only comments; your choice is kept in this browser. New comments no longer go unnoticed.

**Arrange your dashboard**

- Cards are now laid out in rows: the first card on the left, the second on the right, and so on. Cards in the same row are equally tall. On smaller screens they stack in the same order.
- «Notifications» and «From the We.Publish Network» always stay at the top, side by side and the same height. A long network list scrolls inside its card.
- «Customize dashboard» at the top right lets you hide cards, drag them into the order you prefer and show «Latest Activity» across the full width. Your choice is saved in this browser; «Restore default» brings back the original layout.

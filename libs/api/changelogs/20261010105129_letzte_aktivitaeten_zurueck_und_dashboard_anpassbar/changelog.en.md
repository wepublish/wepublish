title: Latest activity is back, and you can arrange your dashboard
lead: The dashboard shows the latest activity again, including new comments. You can now choose which cards you see and in which order.
---

**Latest activity**

The «Latest Activity» card is back: new articles, pages, comments, subscriptions, users, authors, polls and events, each with a link to edit it. New comments no longer go unnoticed.

**Arrange your dashboard**

- Cards are now laid out in rows: the first card on the left, the second on the right, and so on. On smaller screens they stack in the same order.
- «Notifications» and «From the We.Publish Network» always stay at the top, side by side and the same height. A long network list scrolls inside its card.
- «Customize dashboard» at the top right lets you hide cards and drag them into the order you prefer. Your choice is saved in this browser; «Restore default» brings back the original layout.

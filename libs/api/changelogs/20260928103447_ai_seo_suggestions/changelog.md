title: AI suggestions for SEO metadata
lead: In the article and page settings, AI can now suggest the SEO title, SEO description, social media title and description, and the slug.
actionRequired: false
---

The suggestions are based only on the content of the article or page. Nothing is changed until you apply a suggestion, and "Apply to empty fields" never overwrites existing values.

The SEO suggestions use the AI integration that is already configured under Integrations → AI.

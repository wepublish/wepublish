Updates that require a manual step (for example adding a new block to your
navigation) appear as a highlighted notice at the top of the dashboard, like
this one:

![The action-required notice with its Mark as done button](./confirm-flow.svg)

The notice stays visible for your whole team until someone confirms that the
step has been completed:

1. Read what needs to be done — **Details** opens the full description.
2. Complete the step in your instance.
3. Click **Mark as done** and confirm. The notice disappears for everyone, and
   who confirmed it is recorded.

**Your task for this entry:** there is nothing else to set up — simply click
**Mark as done** to try the confirmation flow.

---
title: Letters keep a margin at the top and bottom of every page
lead: The text of a letter now stays at least 2 cm away from the top and the bottom edge of every page. Until now a letter without stationery could run right up to the edge from the second page on.
actionRequired: false
---

**What changes.** On every page of a letter the text keeps at least 2 cm clear of the top and bottom edge, with or without stationery. With stationery the text still keeps clear of the header and footer by 5 mm; a very low header or footer no longer lets the text come closer to the edge than 2 cm.

**What stays.** The address window and the first line of the text on page one are where they were. Appended documents, such as an invoice or a payment slip, are printed unchanged.

**Good to know.** A little less text fits on a page of a letter without stationery, so the page breaks of longer letters move and a letter can become a page longer. Check the preview of letter templates that run over more than one page. Headings also stay with the text that follows them, and a table row or an image is no longer split across two pages.

---
title: Try out the whole payment flow without real money
lead: A new "Simulated" payment provider lets you walk through subscriptions and checkout on a test site. On the checkout page you decide yourself whether the payment succeeds, is declined or is cancelled.
actionRequired: false
---

Set it up under **Settings → Integrations** like any other payment provider, then offer it as a payment method on the membership plans you want to try out.

When a reader reaches the checkout page, three buttons decide what happens:

- **Pay** – the payment goes through and the invoice is marked as paid
- **Decline** – the payment fails
- **Cancel** – the reader abandons the payment

If the provider is set to charge automatically, renewals are booked as paid right away. Tick **Decline renewals** to let them fail instead — useful to see what readers and your team receive when a recurring payment does not go through.

No money ever changes hands, and anyone who opens the checkout page can mark a payment as paid. Use this on test and demo sites only, never on your live site.

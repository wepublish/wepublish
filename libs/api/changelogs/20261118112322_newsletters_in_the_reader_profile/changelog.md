---
title: Readers can choose their newsletters in their profile
lead: Under Communication → Newsletters you set up the Mailchimp newsletters your readers can subscribe to. Logged-in readers then pick them in their profile, and new newsletter forms start with the same newsletters.
actionRequired: false
---

The new **Communication → Newsletters** page lists the newsletters you offer. Each one is an interest of your Mailchimp list, with the name, description and optional image readers see. You can reorder them, and the page needs the Mailchimp sync set up with a list (under Integrations).

Switch on **Show newsletters in the profile** to show them to readers. Logged-in readers then find a "Newsletter" section in their profile, with one card per newsletter and a checkbox. When they tick a newsletter and save, they are subscribed in Mailchimp; new contacts first get a confirmation email from Mailchimp. Switched off, the section is hidden and the subscriptions in Mailchimp stay as they are.

When you add an **Interest selection** to a **Newsletter form** block on the same list, it now starts with these newsletters, so you don't have to enter them again.

---
title: Letters instead of emails, per person
lead: People whose email address is dead or only a placeholder can now get their automated subscription messages as printed letters. You decide who, singly or by audience; the system only suggests.
---

**What changes** (needs the paper-letter flow enabled for your medium)

- **Per person:** the user page shows how automated messages reach them, with a switch to letters and back. Letters need a usable postal address.
- **Per event:** in the subscription flows, each message gets a switch "also as letter". Only switched-on events print; login links, password resets and email confirmations always stay email.
- **By audience:** in the communication tool, "Change delivery of the audience" switches everyone the chosen filters match; people without an address are skipped and counted.
- **Suggestions:** every night the system lists people with a dead or placeholder address who could get letters, and letter recipients whose address works again. Nothing changes until you confirm, one by one or all at once (Communication → Letter suggestions).
- **Print defaults:** the letter integration has print options for automated letters (address window, delivery, duplex, colour).

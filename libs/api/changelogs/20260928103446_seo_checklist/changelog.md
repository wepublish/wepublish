title: SEO checklist
lead: A new SEO checklist under Settings guides you through Google Search Console, sitemaps and feeds, content and the presentation of shared articles. Your team's progress is saved.
actionRequired: false
---

- Each step comes with a short explanation and, where useful, a direct link, for example to Google Search Console or the Google News Publisher Center.
- Tick off steps once they are done. The checklist shows who completed a step and when, for the whole team.
- Some points are checked automatically against your live website: the sitemap, Google News entries, the RSS feed, canonical URLs and structured data of the latest article, and the name and logo of your publication.

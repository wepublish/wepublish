---
title: SEO checklist
lead: A new SEO checklist under Settings shows what We.Publish handles for search engines automatically and what you still have to do.
actionRequired: false
---

The checklist (Settings → SEO) checks your live website for a reachable robots.txt, the sitemap, Google News entries, canonical URLs and NewsArticle structured data. It also checks whether the publication name and logo are set. Steps that have to be done outside We.Publish, such as submitting the sitemap in Google Search Console, are listed separately.

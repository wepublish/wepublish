---
title: Overview lists remember your settings
lead: Filters, sorting and the number of entries per page now stay in place when you reload the editor or return to a list. In the article overview you can also choose which columns are shown.
actionRequired: false
---

**Saved list settings**

Articles, pages, authors, comments, events, images, peer articles, subscriptions, tags, users and discount codes each remember your filter, sort order and page size. When you change a filter or the sorting, you are taken back to the first page so you never land on an empty result page.

**Configurable columns in the article overview**

A new button above the article list lets you show and hide columns. The "Pre-title" column is newly available there; title and status are always shown.

**Rich text editor**

- The text and background colour currently in use are shown as a small colour swatch below the corresponding toolbar icon.
- Resetting the colour of a single word no longer removes the colours from the surrounding paragraph or list.
- The warning about unsaved changes appears less often when nothing was actually edited.

---
title: Lists in forms can be saved again
lead: After adding, editing or removing an entry in a list field, saving did nothing and showed no message. This affected the payment methods and periodicities of subscription plans, the links of authors and the properties of users.
actionRequired: false
---

- Changing the payment methods or periodicities of a subscription plan, for example switching a plan to a lifetime periodicity, can be saved again.
- The same applies to the links of an author and to the properties of a user.

---
title: Text alignment, heading links and a clearer subscription overview
lead: Text alignment you set in the editor now also appears on the website, headings can be linked to directly, and cancelled subscriptions stay in the active list until they actually end.
actionRequired: false
---

**On the website**

- Centred or right-aligned headings and paragraphs are now displayed on the website exactly as you set them in the editor. Previously the alignment was lost when the article was published.
- Headings now carry their anchor, so you can link directly to a section within an article.

**In the member area**

- A subscription that a member has cancelled but that is still running until the end of the paid period now stays under the active subscriptions. It only moves to "cancelled subscriptions" once it has actually ended.

**Accessibility**

- Teasers now announce title, lead and authors to screen readers instead of just the title.

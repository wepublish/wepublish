---
title: Subscribe block shows lifetime plans correctly
lead: In the display mode "One card per plan & interval", the subscribe block now also shows the description of the selected plan, lifetime plans read "one-off" and no longer offer automatic renewal.
actionRequired: false
---

- In "One card per plan & interval", the description and image of the selected subscription plan appear below the cards, as in the other display modes.
- A lifetime offer is labelled "einmalig" (one-off) on its card, whether or not the plan is extendable.
- When a lifetime offer is chosen, the "renew automatically" option is no longer shown, as a lifetime subscription never runs out.

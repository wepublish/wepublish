---
title: Subscribe block shows the full price of each offer
lead: In the display mode "One card per plan & interval", every card now shows the price for the whole interval it stands for, lifetime plans show their full price, and the description of the selected plan appears below the cards.
actionRequired: false
---

- In "One card per plan & interval", each card shows the price of its interval, e.g. the monthly price on a monthly card and the full price on a lifetime card. Previously the cards could show a yearly share, which for a lifetime plan meant its price divided by 100 years.
- Lifetime plans show their full price in the other display modes too.
- The description and image of the selected subscription plan appear below the cards, as in the other display modes.
- A lifetime offer is labelled "einmalig" (one-off) by the default card text, whether or not the plan is extendable.
- Tip: switch off "Extendable" on lifetime plans, so the subscribe form does not offer automatic renewal for them.

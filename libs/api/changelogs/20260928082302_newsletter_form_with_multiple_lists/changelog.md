---
title: Newsletter form with multiple lists
lead: The newsletter form block can now offer several Mailchimp lists at once, so readers pick the newsletters they want to subscribe to.
actionRequired: false
---

In the **Newsletter form** block, the new **Multiple lists** option lets you add several Mailchimp lists. Each list gets a name, a description and an optional image.

You can choose whether readers see the lists as a simple **list** with checkboxes or as a **grid with cards**. Readers must select at least one list before they can submit the form.

Merge fields and interests keep coming from the list selected under **List**. Interests are only submitted if the reader also selects that list.

---
title: Your website now has an llms.txt for AI assistants
lead: Every website now serves a short summary at /llms.txt that helps AI assistants such as ChatGPT or Claude understand your site and find your most important content.
---

`/llms.txt` is a plain-text table of contents written for AI assistants, similar to how the sitemap serves search engines. It is generated automatically and always up to date:

- your site's name and a short description
- one section per navigation, with the pages and links you placed there (or your published pages if there is no navigation)
- your 10 latest articles

Login, signup and profile pages are never listed.

Each article is listed with its first sentence of the SEO description, or of the lead if there is none. A clear SEO description therefore helps AI assistants describe your article correctly.

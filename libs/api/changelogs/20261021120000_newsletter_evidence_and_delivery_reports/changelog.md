---
title: Newsletter bounces count, and you can see what the mail service reports
lead: The email status of your users now also reflects the Mailchimp audience and its campaigns, and the editor tells you when no delivery reports have arrived from the mail service.
actionRequired: false
---

- A contact that Mailchimp cleaned after bounces now counts as "dead" in We.Publish, and a click in a newsletter counts as proof that the address is read. Both come in with the nightly Mailchimp sync.
- Leaving the newsletter no longer makes an address "dead": invoices and other subscription mails still reach it.
- The mail integration shows when the mail service last reported a delivery (webhook) and when We.Publish last asked it. The communication tool and the delivery channel page warn when no report has arrived for a while (setting "Email quality": "Warn without delivery reports after … days", default 14).

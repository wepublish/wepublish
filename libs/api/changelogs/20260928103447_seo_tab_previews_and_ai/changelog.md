lead: The article and page settings have a new SEO tab. It shows how the content looks in Google, on Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Discord and X, and lets the AI suggest metadata and point out optimization potential.
actionRequired: false
---

- **Previews** update live while you edit the SEO and social media texts or change the share image. For X, both the large image card used by the website and the smaller summary card are shown.
- **SEO suggestions**: the AI suggests the SEO title and description, social media title and description, and the slug, based only on the content. Nothing is changed until you apply a suggestion, and "Apply to empty fields" never overwrites existing values.
- **SEO analysis**: the AI reviews the content and metadata and lists concrete improvements, for example a vague title, missing subheadings or images without a description.

The AI features use the AI integration that is already configured under Integrations → AI.

---
title: Email status in the user list
lead: Under Audience → Users you can now show each person's email status, filter by it and sort by it, for example to list every address that no longer receives mail.
actionRequired: false
---

**Two new columns, hidden at first.** Switch them on with the **Columns** button above the table:

- **Email status**: Active, Unknown, Unresponsive or Undeliverable, the same status the communication tool uses. Undeliverable is what the communication tool calls "Dead": the address bounced or is invalid, the person complained about spam, or they unsubscribed at the mail provider. People who have never been rated show as Unknown.
- **Email status since**: the date the address reached its current status. It stays empty for people who have never been rated.

Both columns can be sorted. Sorting by status goes from Active to Undeliverable or the other way round. Sorting by date always lists people without a status last. Both columns are included when you export the table.

**Filter.** Under **Filter** you can pick one or more statuses. People who match any of them are shown. If your letter integration has a placeholder pattern configured, you can also filter by **Placeholder**.

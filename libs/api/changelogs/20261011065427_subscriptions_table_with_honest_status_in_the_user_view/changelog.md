title: Subscriptions at a glance in the user view
lead: A user's subscriptions are now a table with their real status and the invoices of every period. The invoice history lists the newest invoice first, by billing date.
actionRequired: false
---

**Subscriptions in the user view** (Audience → Users → a user)

- **A table instead of cards**, below the user's data and across the full width. Each subscription is one row with its plan, renewal, status, start, periodicity, amount and paid-until date.
- **A status that matches reality.** "Active" now means started and paid beyond today. Otherwise the subscription shows as "Planned", "Unpaid", "Expired" (also without a deactivation, e.g. imported subscriptions that ran out) or "Deactivated". It is the same status the user list shows.
- **Periods under each subscription.** Click a subscription to show its periods, newest first, each with its invoice and whether it is paid.
- **Active subscriptions come first**, then the others by their latest paid-until date.
- **Rearranged cards:** personal data on the left; roles, password and properties on the right; below them the subscriptions, then sending a mail and the mail history side by side.

**Invoice history** (subscription view)

- The date column shows the billing date. For imported invoices that is the start of the period, not the day of the import. The newest invoice comes first; click the column header to reverse the order.

**Invoice numbers on one line**

- Hovering over an invoice number shows it in full on one line, in the invoice history and in the subscriptions table.

---
title: Edit stationery header and footer visually
lead: The header and footer of your stationery can now be edited like a template, visually or as HTML, on a sheet as wide as the printed letter.
actionRequired: false
---

- **Visual or HTML.** Each band of **Stationery** has a «Visual | HTML» switch, as in the template editor. The visual view offers bold, lists, alignment, font size, colour, links and images from the media library.
- **True to size.** The band is shown as wide as an A4 sheet and as high as you set it. A red line marks where the printed band ends; anything below it is not printed.
- Unchanged: the preview still shows a sample letter on your stationery as it will be printed.

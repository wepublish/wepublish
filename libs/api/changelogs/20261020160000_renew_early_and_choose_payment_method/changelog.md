---
title: Renew early and change the payment method in the profile
lead: You decide how long before the end readers can renew their subscription, and readers can now switch their subscription's payment method in their profile at any time, for example from invoice to card.
actionRequired: false
---

**Renewal window**: under **Settings**, "Renewal window (days before the end)" sets how many days before a subscription ends readers see "Renew now" in their profile. 30 keeps the previous behaviour; 366 allows renewing at any time, as long as no invoice is open. The renewal dialog shows which payment method is charged.

**Changing the payment method**: readers choose one of the payment methods the subscription's plan offers for its billing period (**Member plans → Payment methods**). The subscription keeps its current payment method until the new one is confirmed:

- With an open invoice, the reader pays it with the new payment method.
- Without one, the reader confirms an online payment method once with the payment provider. Some providers reserve CHF 0.01 for this; it is released and never charged.
- A payment method without online payment, such as invoice, applies straight away.

Readers whose renewals are charged automatically can also update their current payment method the same way, for example with a new card or TWINT account ("Update current payment method").

While a change is waiting, readers see it in their profile and you see it on the subscription. The open invoice is not charged with the old payment method in the meantime. A change nobody finishes expires after the days set under **Settings** ("Payment method change waiting time", 14 by default), and the subscription keeps its payment method.

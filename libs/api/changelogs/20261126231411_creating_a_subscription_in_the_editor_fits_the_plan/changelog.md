---
title: Creating a subscription in the editor fits the plan
lead: When you pick a plan for a subscription, the periodicity and the amount now follow that plan. A subscription you create on a payment method that never charges is active right away.
actionRequired: false
---

- **Periodicity:** a new subscription takes the plan's default periodicity. An existing subscription keeps its own as long as the new plan offers it. The periodicity list only shows what the plan offers, plus the subscription's current periodicity.
- **Amount:** the amount is the plan's price for the chosen periodicity.
- **Free subscriptions:** on a payment method that never charges, the first invoice is marked paid as soon as the subscription is created, so the subscription is active and paid until the end of its first period. Until now the invoice stayed open, and the subscription was eventually deactivated as unpaid.

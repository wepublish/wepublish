---
title: Article templates: start new articles from a template
lead: You can now save the content and metadata of an article as an article template and start new articles from it.
actionRequired: false
---

Manage your templates under **Articles → Article Templates**. An article template contains blocks, as in the article editor, and metadata such as titles, tags, authors, the paywall and the social media settings.

When you click **New Article**, you can choose between a blank article and one of your article templates. An article created from a template starts as a copy of it. You can edit it freely, and later changes to the template do not affect articles that already exist.

Working with article templates requires the new article template permissions (create, update, delete). The admin and editor roles have them by default. For other roles, your administrator can grant them under user roles.
